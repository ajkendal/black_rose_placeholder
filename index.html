About the league

Real, strong, and open to anyone

Black Rose Rollers is an open-gender flat track roller derby league based in Hanover, Pennsylvania, and a member of the Women's Flat Track Derby Association, the international governing body for the sport. We're a nonprofit, run by our own members, dedicated to an inclusive and welcoming environment for all. Roller derby here is fast, full-contact, and open to everyone, from people lacing up for the first time to seasoned competitors.

We're here to grow the sport in south-central Pennsylvania and give this community a team to rally behind.

Get involved

There's a spot for you

Skate

New skaters are welcome, no experience required. We'll teach you to skate, fall safely, and play. Bring yourself and we'll help with the rest.

Officiate

Every bout needs referees and non-skating officials. If you love the game and want in without the contact, officiating is your way onto the track.

Volunteer

Bouts run on volunteers: setup, scorekeeping, merch, photography and more. Lend a few hours and you're part of the crew.
